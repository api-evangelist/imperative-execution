# Imperative Execution / IntelligentCross

> Imperative Execution is a financial technology company and the parent of IntelligentCross®, an SEC-registered US equities Alternative Trading System (ATS) that uses AI to optimize price discovery and matches orders near-continuously to maximize post-trade price stability.

IntelligentCross operates two matching models: a non-displayed Midpoint book and ASPEN, a full limit order book with optional displayed capability. ASPEN is split into three independent books by fee structure — Fee/Fee, Maker/Taker, and Taker/Maker. All books run under MPID INCR. Primary matching engines are in Equinix NY4 (Secaucus, NJ). Subscribers connect via FIX 4.2 through Pico cross-connects. Clearing is provided by Instinet, LLC.
